Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Keepsake ("we," "us") operates a marketplace that connects people who want photos taken ("customers") with nearby photographers. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Keepsake mobile app and this website.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name, phone number, and email address when you create an account. Your phone number is verified by a text-message code and is how you sign in.

Profile information (photographers). An optional bio, portfolio photos you choose to upload, your service travel radius, and, if you enable the DSLR Pro tier, the make and model of your camera and when it was verified.

Location. For customers: the location you choose for a photo session (typed address or your device location, with your permission). For photographers: while you are marked available, the app periodically reports your approximate location so we only alert you to jobs within your travel radius; while traveling to an accepted job, your location is shared with that customer on a live map until the session starts. We do not track photographers who are off duty, and we never show your location to anyone except a matched customer during an active job.

Photos and photo details. Photos taken during a session (or, on DSLR Pro sessions, imported from the photographer's camera) are delivered to the customer's private album. Each photo is stored with technical details (time taken, device or camera model, image dimensions). See Section 4.

Messages. In-app chat messages between a matched customer and photographer, including read status.

Transactions. Bookings, package prices, tips, cancellation fees, ratings, and reviews. When card payments launch, payment details will be collected and processed by our payment processor. Keepsake does not store full card numbers.

Device information. Push-notification token, device model, and basic app diagnostics.

2. How we use information

  • Match customers with nearby, qualified photographers and dispatch job alerts.
  • Show live arrival tracking and verify the photographer is on location.
  • Deliver photos to the customer and enforce our photographer photo-access lockout (Section 4).
  • Verify DSLR Pro cameras: we read the technical metadata inside a proof photo (camera make, model, and time taken) to confirm a real camera is present.
  • Process bookings, tips, fees, and photographer earnings.
  • Safety, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution.
  • Send service notifications (job status, messages). Marketing messages, if ever sent, will be optional.
  • Comply with law.

3. What other users see

  • Users see each other's first name and last initial only (e.g., "Ryan R."). Full last names never leave our servers.
  • Phone numbers are exchanged only after a photographer accepts your job, and contact options end when the session starts.
  • Customers see a photographer's public profile: short name, bio, portfolio, rating, review history, and (on DSLR jobs) the verified camera model.
  • Reviews you post are public and shown with your short name.

4. Photos: ownership, photographer lockout, and our archive

Your photos go to you. Photos taken in a session are delivered directly to the booking customer's private album.

The photographer loses access. The moment a session's photos are delivered, the photographer's access is revoked, enforced by our servers at multiple layers, and their in-app copies are removed from their device. On standard sessions, photographers cannot keep your photos.

DSLR Pro exception, disclosed at booking. On DSLR Pro sessions the photographer uses their own physical camera, which stores its own copies that Keepsake cannot erase. The app requires you to acknowledge this before booking a DSLR Pro session.

Keepsake keeps an archive copy. We retain a copy of every delivered photo and its technical details in a restricted company archive, accessible only to authorized Keepsake operators, used solely for dispute resolution, fraud prevention, safety investigations, and legal compliance, for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

5. Who we share information with

  • The other party to your job, as described in Section 3.
  • Service providers who run our infrastructure under contract: cloud database and storage, push-notification delivery (via Apple and Google notification services), SMS delivery, payment processing (when payments launch), and photographer background checks and payouts.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the safety of users or the public.
  • Business transfers: if Keepsake is acquired or merges, information may transfer with the business.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location is controlled by your device permissions; photographers can go off-duty at any time.
  • Notifications are controlled in your device settings.
  • Access and deletion: contact us (Section 10) to request a copy or deletion of your data. The safety archive (Section 4) may be retained where legally permitted.

7. Children

Keepsake is not directed to children under 13, and users must be at least 18 to create an account.

8. Security

Photos live in private storage with per-job access rules; every database record is protected by row-level security; all state changes go through validated server functions; names are shortened on our servers before they are ever shared. No system is perfectly secure, but access control here is enforced by the database itself, not just the app.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above; material changes will be announced in the app.
