1. What Keepsake is (and is not)

Keepsake is a marketplace platform that connects people who want photographs taken ("customers") with independent photographers ("photographers"). Keepsake is not a photography studio and does not employ photographers. Photographers are independent contractors who decide when to be available, which jobs to accept, and how far they will travel.

3. Booking, pricing, and payment

  • Tips are optional, go 100% to the photographer, and are non-refundable once the session is complete.

  • Photographers: cancelling an accepted job returns it to other photographers and results in reduced job priority for 7 days, plus a permanent block on re-taking the abandoned job.
  • "Now" jobs a photographer accepts but does not begin within the app's stated window are automatically released to other photographers.

5. The session

The photographer travels to the customer's chosen location; live location is shared with the customer en route. Sessions can only start when the photographer is physically at the location (verified by the app). The session lasts the purchased duration and ends automatically; photo capture after the paid window is not accepted by our systems. Both parties agree to behave lawfully and respectfully. Customers must have the right to be photographed at the chosen location; photographers must comply with local rules at the location.
